Client Challenge

Technical Disconnect & Critical Exhibition Deadline

Industry: Engineering Company

The client was developing electrical components for an industrial automation system. They wanted to protect their components through patents. They spent a month explaining their invention to standard advocates who lacked technical backgrounds and could not comprehend the technical language.

Frustrated and facing a critical deadline, the client approached Excelon IP with extreme urgency: they were scheduled to showcase the product at an international exhibition in just **one week** and needed a patent filed beforehand.

Excelon IP Solution

Excelon IP responded immediately by dispatching technical experts to the client's manufacturing site. We:

  • Conducted on-site technical reviews to fully understand the product operation and novel features in their exact engineering terms.
  • Conducted prior art searches to confirm the novelty of the automation circuits.
  • Drafted the complete patent specifications, coordinating 2-3 interactive technical iterations with the client, and filed the patent on the **5th day** following our first meeting.

Business Impact & Benefits

Successful Exhibition Launch & Patent Pending Moat

The client was amazed by our technical efficiency and speed of execution. They showcased their brand-new automation components at the exhibition under a secure **"PATENT PENDING"** status, which protected their technology from cloning by competitors and generated high-quality business leads during the show.
