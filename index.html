Invention Disclosure

Capturing the initial technical architecture, drawings, and functional claims of your innovation.

Required Documents & Inputs

  • Technical description document (code, formulas, flowcharts)
  • Signed inventor disclosure forms
  • Signed NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) with team

Excelon IP Strategy Tip

Do not disclose your invention publicly (on social media, journals, or pitching to investors without NDA) before filing, as public disclosure invalidates novelty requirements globally.

Case Studies & Precedents

Real battles, expert outcomes. Explore some of the high-profile patent disputes analyzed by our experts.

Infringement

Apple vs. Masimo

The Big Bite: Apple's smart watch sales were halted temporarily due to blood oxygen sensor patent infringement claims brought by medical sensor developer Masimo.

Conflict: Infringement of pulse oximeter hardware utilities inside mass consumer devices.
Outcome: Import bans enforced; highlights importance of patent-first R&D for tech brands.

Pharma Patent

Bayer vs. Natco Pharma

A legal dispute concerning the cancer drug Regorafenib, focusing on innovators' exclusive monopoly patents vs. generic manufacturers' generic substitutions.

Conflict: Patents covering Regorafenib formulations and generic equivalents access pricing.
Outcome: Redefined pharmaceutical patent landscape and drug accessibility pathways.

Patent Moat

Novartis vs. Natco Pharma

High-stakes patent dispute on life-saving leukemia medications, evaluating regulatory boundaries for evergreening of compound patents.

Conflict: Evergreening attempts on incremental chemical modifications.
Outcome: Landmark court precedent limiting patent expansions to true novel breakthroughs.

Shark Tank / Utility

Zerodor's Waterless Urinal

Shark Tank India's feature highlighted Zerodor's waterless mechanical solutions, driving questions about patent moat utility filings.

Conflict: Validation of mechanical non-return valve utility patents.
Outcome: Demonstrated the strength of active patent defense for startup pitches on television.
