Novelty & Patentability Searches

Novelty Search Services

A novelty search is a quick and rapid search performed in various databases before starting with the drafting of a patent. It aims to identify any prior art which would knock out the novelty feature of an idea. Our designated team of experts can provide you with a comprehensive novelty search report.

Patentability Search Services

A distinguished patentability search is an important step for an invention as it helps decide whether such an invention can be patented or not. A thorough search identifies the closest patent to help evaluate if the novelty of the invention clashes with the prior art. If the invention stands for novelty, the claims will be drafted around the closest patent so that the novel features are easily identifiable and defensible.

State of Art & Markush Structures

State of Art Search Services

To enter into a particular industry, it is important for the inventor to know about the industry and the current state of technology in the targeted field. State of Art search helps understand the current technological levels of the industry from an aerial perspective, which helps the inventor make informed decisions about their technical strategy.

Markush Structure Assessment (Molecular Structure Search)

A Markush structure is a depiction of a chemical structure that is used to show related compounds. While filing a patent, pharma companies prepare general claims for using a molecule without disclosing the exact molecular structures to competitors, keeping key practical applications confidential. Our team conducts molecular structure databases matching to clear these complex filings.
