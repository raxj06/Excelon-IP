Patent Analyst (Life Sciences / Pharma)

Ahmedabad (HQ)
Full Time
Job Overview

We are seeking a detail-oriented Patent Analyst to handle prior art searching, patent drafting, and FTO assessments in pharmaceutical, biochemistry, and life sciences domains.

Key Responsibilities
  • Conduct prior-art patentability, invalidity, and landscape searches on global databases (USPTO, EPO, WIPO, IPO).
  • Draft detailed patent specifications conforming to Indian and PCT standards.
  • Support patent agents in drafting FER (First Examination Report) responses.
Requirements
  • M.Pharm / M.Sc. in Biotechnology, Organic Chemistry, or related sciences.
  • 1-3 years of patent searching and analytics experience.

Trademark Associate / Legal Attorney

Ahmedabad / Remote
Full Time
Job Overview

We are seeking a trademark attorney to manage clearance analytics, registration prosecution, and opponent representation before the IP registries.

Key Responsibilities
  • Conduct phonetic and visual clearance searches for brands and design marks.
  • Draft responses to Examiner objections, TM oppositions, and show cause statements.
  • Represent clients during registry hearings and manage cease & desist actions.
Requirements
  • LL.B. / LL.M. with active bar enrollment.
  • 2+ years of dedicated trademark prosecution experience.
