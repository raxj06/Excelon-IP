Coloring Chemistry, Green Chemistry & IP

Dyes and colouring pigments are used as a solution which sticks to wherever it is applied and gives an aesthetic look to the article and serves other artistic purposes. The dyes and pigment industry is directly or indirectly connected with Paints & Coatings, Textile, Printing Ink, Plastic, and other end-user industries. Dyes also make specific cells and organs clearer and more visible for medical imaging. Dye lasers can also be used for vaporising tattoo inks.

Dyes and pigments are currently in a boom as each and every product we use in our day-to-day life is created with the use of one or another chemical, dye, or pigment. Further, due to shutdowns in many Chinese dye and pigment plants because of pollution, this industry in other regions is getting good traction. However, this is not permanent, and environmental factors are always a risk.

To tackle environmental risk, a whole new vertical is opening in green chemistry and green processes, which has tremendous scope for innovation and IP creation. Further, there are many benefits to filing IP for the green process.

Client Challenge

Competitor Infringement Notice Defense

Industry: Dye & Pigment Manufacturing Company

The client, an established dye and color pigment manufacturing company, received a formal legal notice from a third-party competitor claiming patent infringement. The competitor alleged that the client's manufacturing processes and dye compositions infringed their active patent, threatening to halt the client's plant operations.

Excelon IP Solution

Excelon IP's chemical patent analysts evaluated the legal and technical scope of the competitor's patent. We:

  • Conducted an in-depth claim mapping audit comparing the competitor's patent claims with the client's actual manufacturing processes and chemical products.
  • Discovered that the client's products did not fall under the competitor's claimed scope.
  • Drafted a robust, technical non-infringement legal reply outlining evidence-backed structural and process differences.

Business Impact & Benefits

Successful Defense & Continuous Operations

Following the submission of our detailed technical rebuttal, the third party ceased all claims and did not pursue further action. The client was cleared of liability and successfully continued manufacturing and shipping their dye products globally without any legal hurdles.
