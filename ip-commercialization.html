What is IP Commercialization?

The worth of an IP asset lies in the commercialization of such assets and not necessarily in creation. Mergers and acquisitions originate because of the expertise and the intellectual property rights reserved by the big entities, representing a dominance of IP in the industry.

A business entity acquires a competitive edge only by putting its creation to use—that is, commercializing its IP assets. Excelon IP is one of the best IP Commercialization Services in India. Commercialization of IP helps to bring in investments. It is necessary for a company to realize the importance of such assets, commercialize them in time, and reap the fruits of their own creations. This escalates the bright future of a business entity.

Consequently, the commercialization of intellectual property is a necessary activity as it allows the business entity or the creator to seek his/her share from society in exchange for providing access to their creations to the people at a wider level.

How to Commercialize Your IP Assets?

Commercialization can be achieved for intellectual property assets either by assigning the IP rights or by licensing them.

Assignment of IP Rights

Assignment of rights is similar to selling a tangible property, wherein it involves the complete transfer of ownership rights from one person or entity to another. Once assigned, the original creator holds no future claim or control over the asset.

Licensing of IP Rights

Licensing of IP rights means withholding the ownership rights and giving away the rights of use to some other person or entity on agreed terms for a limited period of time. Under licensing, ownership rights are retained by the proprietor of such intellectual property assets.

The Strategic Benefits of Licensing

In order to license your IP assets, it is a prerequisite to have a strategy for the maximum benefit of the holder of the IP assets.

By licensing, the creator can generate much-needed revenue which can be further used for the creation, development, and production of new IP assets. Considering the benefits from a marketing angle, the licensor expands their reach across global markets.

Strategically, the licensor saves the heavy costs of production, distribution channels, marketing, and other associated expenditures, which would otherwise be a heavy burden. Licensing also facilitates futuristic relationships between licensors and licensees, creating opportunities for larger joint ventures and collaborative research partnerships.
